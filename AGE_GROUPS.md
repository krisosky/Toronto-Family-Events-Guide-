# AGE_GROUPS.md

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: 2026-10-28

Owner: Family Events Guide

## Purpose

This document defines the official age classification system used by Family Events Guide.

All events must be assigned at least one age group whenever possible.

AGE_GROUPS.md is the official source of truth for all age classifications used by Family Events Guide.

All website pages, event records, organizer submissions, OpenClaw skills, newsletters and search filters must use these age groups unless formally updated.

---

# OFFICIAL AGE GROUPS

## Baby & Infant

Age Range:

0-12 months

Examples:

* baby story time
* infant sensory play
* parent-and-baby music
* infant swim programs
* stroller-friendly activities

---

## Toddler

Age Range:

1-3 years

Examples:

* toddler gym
* music and movement
* early learning activities
* toddler story time
* parent-and-toddler programs

---

## Preschool

Age Range:

3-5 years

Examples:

* preschool art
* beginner sports
* preschool STEM
* early literacy programs
* music and movement

---

## Early Elementary

Age Range:

5-7 years

Examples:

* junior robotics
* beginner theatre
* beginner coding
* elementary sports
* craft workshops

---

## Elementary

Age Range:

8-10 years

Examples:

* coding classes
* science workshops
* team sports
* art programs
* museum workshops

---

## Tween

Age Range:

10-12 years

Examples:

* advanced STEM
* leadership programs
* competitive sports
* creative writing
* pre-teen workshops

---

## Teen

Age Range:

13-17 years

Examples:

* volunteer programs
* youth leadership
* teen recreation
* teen workshops
* career exploration programs

---

## Parent/Caregiver

Primary Audience:

Adults supporting children.

Examples:

* parenting workshops
* family support groups
* caregiver education
* school readiness seminars
* parent information nights

---

## All Ages

Suitable for multiple generations or broad family participation.

Examples:

* festivals
* community events
* holiday celebrations
* outdoor movie nights
* family fairs
* parades

---

## Unknown

Use only when age suitability cannot be determined.

Events assigned Unknown must be flagged:

Status = Needs Review

Unknown should not be used for published events unless reviewed and approved by a human.

---

# ASSIGNMENT RULES

## Organizer Provides Age Range

Use the organizer-provided age range.

## Organizer Provides Grade Level

Convert the grade level to the closest age group.

## Organizer Provides “All Ages”

Assign:

All Ages

## No Age Information Exists

Assign:

Unknown

Status:

Needs Review

## Do Not Guess

Never guess age suitability without evidence.

Use Unknown and flag for review instead.

---

Events may belong to multiple age groups.

Family Festival

* Elementary
* Tween
* Teen
